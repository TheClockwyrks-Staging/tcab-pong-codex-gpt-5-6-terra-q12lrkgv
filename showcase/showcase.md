# Carom

Carom is a bright, fast paddle duel played across a midnight field. Every rally starts cleanly at center, then turns into a contest of angles, timing, and nerve.

Slide your paddle as the ball arrives to bend its next flight. A well-timed hit can curl a return into a wall, slip it past a defender, or set up a sharp bank.

The twin center blocks turn the court into a pocket of possibilities. Learn the lanes, improvise under pressure, and close out the final two-point lead.
